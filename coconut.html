🥥 Coconut

Coconut is an important plantation crop with multiple uses across food, agriculture and value-added industries.

🌱 Cultivation

Healthy planting material, suitable soil, proper spacing, irrigation and regular farm management are important for productive coconut farming.

💧 Water Management

Efficient irrigation and moisture conservation can help maintain healthy coconut palms, especially during dry periods.

🌿 Farm Management

Nutrition management, weed control, mulching, pest monitoring and regular field maintenance are important parts of coconut farming.

🥥 Value & Uses

Coconut provides opportunities through tender coconut, mature nuts, copra, coconut oil and other value-added products.

🚜 SR CRESCO Focus

SR CRESCO aims to promote practical knowledge, sustainable farming and modern approaches for better agricultural opportunities.
