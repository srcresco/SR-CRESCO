🏛️ Government Schemes

Government agricultural schemes can provide farmers with support through financial assistance, infrastructure development, insurance, irrigation and other agricultural services.

🌱 What Farmers Can Look For

💰 Financial Support
Information about eligible agricultural financial assistance and support programmes.
🛡️ Crop Insurance
Farmers can explore applicable crop insurance programmes to manage agricultural risks.
💧 Irrigation Support
Information about irrigation and water-management programmes may help farmers improve farm water resources.
🚜 Farm Equipment
Eligible farmers may find support programmes related to agricultural machinery and equipment.
🌾 Farmer Development
Various programmes may support farming infrastructure, productivity, training and agricultural development.

📌 Important

Scheme eligibility, benefits, application procedures and deadlines can change. Farmers should always verify the latest information through the official government department or portal before applying.
