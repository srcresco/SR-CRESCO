🚁 Drone Technology

Agricultural drones can help farmers monitor fields, collect aerial information and support precision farming practices.

🌱 Crop Monitoring

Drones can capture aerial images of agricultural fields, helping farmers observe crop growth and identify areas that may need attention.

📍 Field Mapping

Drone-based mapping can provide detailed information about farm boundaries, crop areas and field conditions.

💧 Irrigation Management

Aerial monitoring can help identify differences in crop and field conditions that may support better irrigation planning.

🧪 Precision Agriculture

Drone data can be combined with other farm information to support more targeted and efficient agricultural decisions.

🚜 SR CRESCO Focus

SR CRESCO aims to promote practical applications of drone technology for smarter, data-driven and sustainable agriculture.
