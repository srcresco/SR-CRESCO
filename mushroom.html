🍄 Mushroom

Mushroom cultivation is a space-efficient agricultural activity that can provide opportunities for farm diversification and value-added production.

🌱 Cultivation

Successful mushroom production depends on suitable growing conditions, quality substrate, hygiene and proper temperature and moisture management.

💧 Growing Conditions

Maintaining appropriate humidity, ventilation and temperature is important for healthy mushroom development.

🧼 Hygiene

Clean growing areas and proper handling practices help reduce contamination and support consistent production.

📦 Value Addition

Fresh mushrooms can also be processed into different value-added food products, creating additional market opportunities.
