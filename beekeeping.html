🐝 Beekeeping

Beekeeping is an agricultural activity focused on maintaining honeybee colonies for honey production and pollination support.

🍯 Honey Production

Healthy bee colonies can produce honey while providing opportunities for farmers through value-added honey products.

🌸 Pollination

Honeybees play an important role in pollination and can support the productivity of many flowering crops.

🐝 Colony Management

Regular monitoring, suitable hive placement and proper colony management are important for maintaining healthy bee colonies.

🌱 Sustainable Farming

Beekeeping can complement crop farming and contribute to diversified and sustainable agricultural activities.

🚜 SR CRESCO Focus

SR CRESCO aims to share practical knowledge and promote agricultural opportunities connected with beekeeping and sustainable farming.
