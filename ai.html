🤖 AI in Agriculture

Artificial Intelligence can help farmers use agricultural data to make smarter and more informed farming decisions.

🌱 Crop Monitoring

AI-based systems can analyse crop and field information to help identify changes in crop condition and support timely decisions.

💧 Smart Irrigation

Data-driven systems can help farmers understand crop water needs and improve irrigation planning.

🐛 Pest & Disease Monitoring

AI tools can assist in detecting possible crop stress, pests and disease symptoms using images and agricultural data.

📊 Farm Data

Combining weather, soil, crop and field data can provide useful insights for planning and farm management.

🚜 SR CRESCO Focus

SR CRESCO aims to explore practical applications of AI and modern technology to support smarter, sustainable agriculture.
