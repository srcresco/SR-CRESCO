🌰 Macadamia

Macadamia is a premium nut crop with potential for long-term orchard development and value-added agricultural opportunities.

🌱 Cultivation

Macadamia cultivation requires suitable climate, well-drained soil, quality planting material and proper orchard management.

💧 Water Management

Efficient irrigation and moisture management are important, particularly during establishment and dry periods.

🌿 Orchard Management

Nutrition, weed management, pruning and regular monitoring can support healthy tree growth and productive orchards.

🥜 Value Addition

Macadamia nuts can be used in food products and processed into different value-added products, creating additional market opportunities.
