🥑 Avocado

Avocado is a high-value fruit crop with growing demand in fresh markets and food industries.

🌱 Cultivation

Successful avocado farming depends on suitable climate, well-drained soil, proper irrigation and good farm management.

💧 Water Management

Regular and balanced irrigation is important, while avoiding waterlogging around the root zone.

🌿 Farm Management

Good planting material, nutrition, mulching, pruning and regular monitoring can support healthy plant growth.

📈 Market Opportunity

Avocado can provide opportunities for farmers through fresh fruit production, value addition and direct market connections.
