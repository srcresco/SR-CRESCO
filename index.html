🌾 About SR CRESCO

SR CRESCO — Grow • Prosper • Thrive

SR CRESCO is a modern agriculture platform focused on smart farming, sustainable agriculture, agriculture technology, farmer knowledge, agricultural products and farmer community in Karnataka, India.

We explore agriculture opportunities including avocado, coconut, macadamia, mushroom cultivation, beekeeping, dairy farming and smart agriculture technologies.

SR CRESCO aims to connect agriculture with knowledge, innovation and technology for a smarter and more sustainable future of farming.

Founded by Subhash V S
Chamarajanagar, Karnataka, India
