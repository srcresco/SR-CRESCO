🛰️ Satellite Monitoring

Satellite technology can provide valuable information about agricultural fields and support modern farm monitoring.

🌱 Crop Health

Satellite imagery can help observe changes in vegetation and identify areas that may require closer field inspection.

💧 Water & Moisture Insights

Remote sensing information can support understanding of field conditions and help improve agricultural planning.

📍 Farm Monitoring

Large agricultural areas can be monitored using satellite imagery without visiting every part of the field regularly.

📊 Data-Based Farming

Satellite information can be combined with weather, soil and field data to support better agricultural decisions.

🚜 SR CRESCO Focus

SR CRESCO aims to explore satellite-based solutions for smarter, data-driven and sustainable agriculture.
