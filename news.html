📅 8 September 2026

🌱 National Horticulture Board Subsidy Applications Temporarily Suspended

The National Horticulture Board has temporarily suspended fresh applications under two horticulture-related schemes for one month from September 4, 2026.

The schemes include support for commercial horticulture production and post-harvest management, along with assistance for construction, expansion and modernization of cold storage facilities.

Farmers, entrepreneurs and agriculture businesses planning horticulture or cold-storage projects should check the latest official guidelines before submitting new applications.

📅 8 September 2026

🤖 AI and Agriculture: Technology Supporting Smarter Farming

Artificial intelligence is increasingly being explored alongside traditional farming practices to improve agricultural decision-making.

AI models can use satellite imagery and agricultural data to help understand crop conditions, water availability and changes across farming areas.

Such technologies could support farmers with more targeted information and better resource management.

📅 8 September 2026

🌾 Value Addition Creates New Opportunities for Pulses

Agricultural innovation is also focusing on adding value to locally produced crops.

A Bangalore-based agricultural innovation centre has developed ready-to-eat energy bars using pulses from Kalaburagi, demonstrating how farm produce can be converted into higher-value food products.

Value addition can create new opportunities for farmers, food entrepreneurs and rural businesses.

📅 8 September 2026

🌿 ICAR Training Focuses on Nursery Management and Grafting

ICAR-IIVR in Varanasi has started a five-day hands-on training programme focusing on nursery management and grafting techniques in vegetable crops.

The programme is designed to provide practical skills to farmers and strengthen knowledge of improved plant propagation techniques.

📅 7 September 2026

🌱 National Conference on Natural Farming 2026

The National Conference on Natural Farming 2026 is scheduled to be held from September 9 to 11 in Meghalaya.

The conference is expected to bring together farmers, agricultural institutions, researchers and other stakeholders to discuss natural farming practices, innovation and sustainable agriculture.

📅 6 September 2026

🛰️ Satellite Technology Supporting Modern Agriculture

Satellite-based monitoring is becoming an important tool in modern agriculture.

Satellite imagery can help monitor vegetation, crop conditions, water resources and changes in agricultural land over large areas.

Combining satellite data with field-level information and farmer knowledge can support more informed agricultural decisions.
